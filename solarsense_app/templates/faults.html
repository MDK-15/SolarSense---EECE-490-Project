How does fault detection work?

We use a neural network (autoencoder) trained on thousands of healthy solar and battery cycles. It learns what normal operation looks like. When you upload your data, it checks whether your readings match the normal pattern. Large deviations are flagged as potential faults. We also run additional rule-based checks for specific known fault types like overvoltage.

Important: These results are informational and should not replace professional inspection. If a fault is detected, contact a certified solar installer or electrician.
